# Visual Direction Presets

Use these after visual direction is settled to translate the chosen shape into concrete implementation cues. They are not a substitute for `design-direction` when intent is still unresolved.

## Product calm

- clear hierarchy, restrained surfaces, low noise
- one dominant action, quiet supporting content
- good for settings, account flows, and task-focused tools

## Control room

- denser information layout, stronger grouping, obvious status cues
- good for operational dashboards and review surfaces
- avoid decorative cards that do not carry meaning

## Editorial clarity

- wider rhythm, readable type, fewer competing accents
- good for long-form content, guides, and decision summaries
- keep composition deliberate rather than ornamental

## Mobile touch

- larger touch targets, clear stacking, minimal precision work
- good for mobile-first or touch-heavy tasks
- keep the primary path obvious without crowding the viewport

## Focused handoff

- one clear focal element, explicit secondary context, minimal branching
- good for screens that must be easy to review and carry forward
- useful when the next step is implementation rather than exploration

## Use notes

- Start from the preset already chosen in the brief or `## Design Direction` artifact, then adapt it to the actual task.
- Do not combine presets just because they exist.
- If the chosen preset must survive downstream work unchanged, capture it in `## Design Handoff Context` with `design-handoff`.
